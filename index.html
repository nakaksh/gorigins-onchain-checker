Old Gorigins Wallet Checker

Enter a public Gorbagana wallet address. This page reads its current finalized token accounts directly from Gorbagana. It never connects to your wallet and cannot request a signature or transaction.

    How to verify

    A displayed Gulag #174 corresponds to original Gorigin #174 and restored Gorigin #174. Compare the number, mint and wallet with the frozen community allocation JSON.

    This page shows NFTs held directly by the wallet now. An NFT deposited into marketplace escrow is held by the escrow account and will not appear under the seller's wallet here.
